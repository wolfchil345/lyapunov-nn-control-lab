# References and Further Reading

## Control and stability

- State-space modeling, feedback control, and Linear Quadratic Regulator design.
- Lyapunov direct method, quadratic Lyapunov functions, and region-of-attraction analysis.
- Robust and nonlinear control for systems with uncertainty and input constraints.

## Learning-based control

- Neural-network controllers and imitation learning.
- Stability-aware losses and neural Lyapunov methods.
- Safe reinforcement learning and formal verification of neural controllers.
- Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks for control-oriented function approximation.

## Numerical methods

- Initial-value ODE solvers, grid-based evaluation, sensitivity analysis, and uncertainty sweeps.
