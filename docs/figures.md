# Figures Guide

This guide explains the generated figures in the `results/` directory.

New figures are stored inside `results/runs/<run_id>/` with a verified
`manifest.json` and `SHA256SUMS`. Root-level PNG files are preserved
legacy/unverified historical artifacts; see [`../results/README.md`](../results/README.md).

Future figures use normalized position, velocity, time, control, and state-norm
labels. The committed PNG files are historical release artifacts and are not
regenerated by the coordinate-semantics change; some therefore retain older
generic labels or `Time [s]` wording.

## Main comparison plots

### `position_comparison.png`
Compares the position response of the LQR controller and the neural-network controller.

### `training_loss.png`
Shows the neural-network training loss over epochs.

### `multiple_initial_conditions.png`
Shows how the neural-network controller behaves from several different initial states.

## Stability and Lyapunov plots

### `phase_portrait.png`
Shows trajectories in the position-velocity state space.

### `lyapunov_contours.png`
Shows Lyapunov function contours together with closed-loop trajectories.

### `finite_horizon_convergence.png`
Shows which sampled initial states satisfy `||x(T)||_2 < epsilon` for the stated
normalized-time horizon, normalized-state tolerance, bounds, and grid. It is not an asymptotic attraction-region
calculation.

### `finite_horizon_convergence_comparison.png`
Compares the same finite-time final-state criterion across controllers and
reports converged/tested counts and percentages.

The tracked `region_of_attraction.png` and
`region_of_attraction_comparison.png` files are historical artifacts generated
before the terminology correction. They remain unchanged; future runs use the
two corrected filenames above.

## Robustness plots

### `saturation_comparison.png`
Compares controller behavior when control input limits are applied.

### `noise_robustness.png`
Historical single-seed figure retained unchanged. Future paired experiments
write `noise_robustness_paired.png` for aggregate final-state statistics and
`noise_robustness_paired_trajectories.png` for representative matched-seed
trajectories.

### `parameter_robustness.png`
Shows how the controller behaves when normalized model coefficients are changed.

## Architecture and ablation plots

### `model_architecture.png`
Shows the project workflow from plant model to controller, simulation, stability checks, and reports.

### `stability_weight_ablation.png`
Historical figure retained unchanged. Future paired experiments write
`stability_weight_ablation_paired.png`, showing faint per-seed observations,
mean trends, and sample variability where multiple repeats are available.

## Data files

### `performance_metrics.csv`
Stores numerical performance metrics for the controllers.

### `stability_weight_ablation.csv`
Historical results from the earlier confounded seed design. Future paired runs
write separate `stability_weight_ablation_trials_paired.csv` and
`stability_weight_ablation_summary_paired.csv` files. Noise trials and summaries
follow the corresponding `noise_robustness_*_paired.csv` naming convention.

### `experiment_report.md`
Automatically summarizes the generated plots, metrics, and experiments.
